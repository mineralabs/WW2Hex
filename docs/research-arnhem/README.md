# Research for the Arnhem scenario

Gathered on 16-17 October 2026 from published histories, war diaries transcribed online and open map
data, for the Arnhem scenario ([../arnhem-notes.md](../arnhem-notes.md)). All of it is provisional:
much was read through online copies, search extracts or secondary works, and each file lists the
conflicts and open questions to check against the printed sources (Middlebrook, Kershaw, Margry).

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [allied-forces.md](allied-forces.md) | The 1st British Airborne Division on 17-18 September: units, landing zones, strengths, equipment, routes, the bridge force, the second lift, the 18 September resupply, timeline and suggested victory conditions |
| [german-forces.md](german-forces.md) | Krafft, Spindler's blocking line, Gräbner, Brinkmann, Knaust and Mielke at the bridge, Division von Tettau, entry routes, supply, timeline and a counter list |
| [geography-1944.md](geography-1944.md) | Rivers, crossings and their state, settlements, woods and heaths, heights, zones, post-war changes, map sources, and the ready-to-use map-config lists |
| [settlements-roads-1944.md](settlements-roads-1944.md) | Built-up areas and roads as they were in 1944 |

**Clock.** Times are Allied "A" time (GMT+1), as in the Allied orders and diaries. German
records ran an hour later (GMT+2); German-clock times were moved an hour earlier for the scenario.
