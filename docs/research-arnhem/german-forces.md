# German Order of Battle: Arnhem, 17–18 September 1944

Scenario window: 13:00 on 17 Sep 1944 (first British lift lands west of Arnhem) to about 21:00 on 18 Sep 1944. That is 16 turns of 2 hours.
Map: the landing zones at Ginkel Heath, Renkum and Wolfheze (W) to Velp and Westervoort (E), and Deelen and Schaarsbergen (N) to the Nederrijn south bank at Driel, Elden and Malburgen (S). About 1 km hexes, battalion or Kampfgruppe counters.

Researched October 2026. This is a research draft. Check it against the books in section 12 before the scenario data is final.

---

## 0. How to read this file

**Confidence labels.** Location (Loc), Strength (Str) and Equipment (Eq) each get their own label:

- **V (verified):** at least two independent sources agree, or one well-referenced source (one that cites German war diaries or reports) has no serious conflict.
- **P (provisional):** one source only, derivative sources only, or the sources disagree on the detail.
- **U (unknown):** no source found. Any figure shown is an *estimate*, and the basis is given.

**Source independence.** Many web sources go back to the same few books. Nafziger [NAF] cites only Kershaw. The marketgarden.com armour list [MG9] follows Zwarts, *German Armour in Arnhem*. Middlebrook [MID] credits Kershaw for the Gräbner details. Two sources from the same root are not counted as two for **V**. Where that is the case, the entry says so.

**Coordinates.** WGS84 decimal degrees, to about ±300 m in built-up areas. Assembly areas and lines are about ±1 km. They were taken from OpenStreetMap Nominatim look-ups of the modern named feature: the hotel, station, road, crossroads or village. Where a unit stood "east of X" or "along road Y", I offset the point by eye from the look-up and marked it *est.* None comes from a 1944 grid reference. Lines are given as a chain of points.

**Clock times.** The 1944 British and German clocks were probably one hour apart, though this was not checked source by source. Germany kept summer time (GMT+2). Britain's double summer time is believed to have ended on 17 Sep 1944, putting British clocks on GMT+1. A German "18:00" may therefore be a British "17:00". [DA] notes one German time explicitly ("0615h (German time)"). British-sourced times ([MID], [PA], [WP]) are probably British clock. German-sourced times ([TIE], [DA] from Krafft's diary, [NAF]) are probably German clock. **The scenario should state which clock it uses.** This file assumes the 13:00 start is British time, so German-clock times should be read about one hour early.

**Map edge.** The map limits had not been fixed when this was written. "Off-map" below means outside the box above.

## 6. 10th SS "Frundsberg" crossing at Pannerden (as far as it matters for Arnhem)

- **Routing:** at about 16:00 on 17 Sep the 10th SS was ordered to Nijmegen *via Arnhem* [TIE]. It found the bridge blocked. At about midnight II SS-Pz.Korps rerouted it **SE of Arnhem across the Nederrijn by ferry at Pannerden** [TIE]. The leading elements were brought to a halt east of Velp [TIE].
- **Crossings on 18 Sep:** the pioneer company Baumgärtel and Btl. Euling crossed early in the morning, KG Reinhold early in the afternoon [NAF]. Pioneer Company "Brandt & Munski" built the ferry [NAF]. A 10th SS flak company was at Pannerden on 18 Sep for ferry air defence [NAF], [TIE].
- **Relevance to the scenario:** these units **leave** the Arnhem fight. Pannerden (51.890, 6.037) and the ferry are off-map SE. Their route to Nijmegen (Pannerden → Doornenburg → Bemmel → Elst/Lent) runs mostly south of this map; it crosses the map only if the south edge reaches Elst. Within the window, the only 10th SS units that matter at Arnhem are **Brinkmann, 3./21, Euling's forward elements and one battery** (section 4).
- **South bank in the window:** Gräbner's survivors and the two naval companies at the S end of the bridge; flak or MGs on the south bank at **Meinerswijk** (an "88 mm" is reported firing along the Onderlangs on 18 Sep [BOA-S18]); and "German positions in a factory on the south bank" firing on 3 Para along the river path [MID p.171]. Parent unit unknown. *Recommendation:* one south-bank flak/MG counter at Meinerswijk (51.975, 5.884), with Loc P / Str U.
- **Not in the window:** SS-Pz.Rgt.10's Pz IVs, SS-PGR 21 (main body, 19 Sep onward) and SS-PGR 22 went to the Betuwe and Nijmegen (see the Nijmegen file).

From 2 Para's arrival onwards, **no German traffic crossed the bridge** until Frost's force was overwhelmed on 20/21 Sep (outside the window). This is the main German operational problem of the scenario.

### 9.2 German supply in the area

- **9th SS:** support units at **Velp and Apeldoorn** [NAF]. Supply arrived over the Velp and Apeldoorn roads. Units were short of heavy weapons but not, as far as was found, of small-arms ammunition. Krafft spent 18 Sep replenishing "fuel, munitions and rations" [DA-K]. Later his mortars could fire 50 rounds a day thanks to "well organised munition supply" [DA-K]. The many local higher HQs (A Gp B, WBN, the HSSPF, II SS-Pz.Korps) "sped up the logistics supply chain"; units drew directly from them [DA-OOB]. Captured British air-dropped supplies became important from 19 Sep (DZ "V" and "L" fell to the Germans) [WP], [BOA-HZ].
- **10th SS:** supply column at **Didam**, with the flak battery CP at Zevenaar [TIE I]. Pannerden ferry capacity limited the 10th SS south of the river (see the Nijmegen file).
- **Tettau's units:** drawn from the WBN area in the W Netherlands. Poor transport: bicycles, commandeered lorries [DA-WB], [DA-HG]. Lippert had "no transport" [MG7].
- **Rail:** railheads at **Zevenaar** (Mielke; s.Pz.Abt.506 later) and **Bocholt** (Hummel). Rail into Arnhem was blocked or cut [MIKE], [DA-HUM].
- **Game suggestion:** German supply sources at the N edge (Apeldoorn road), the NE/E edge (Velp road and Westervoort bridge) and the W edge (Ede road). Tettau units trace supply W only, not to the 9th SS sources, until the 20 Sep command merger (outside the window). The south-bank units (G-AA9, G-NAV-S) trace S to Elst. They are cut off from the north while the British hold the N end of the bridge.

## 11. What a historical result at 21:00 on 18 Sep looks like (German side)

- **Road bridge:** **not** in German hands at the north end. Frost's force (about 740 men at the start, fewer by now) holds a block of houses around the N ramp. It is surrounded on the N and E by Brinkmann, Knaust, Mielke and Sonnenstuhl's groups, and on the S bank by Gräbner's survivors and the naval companies. **No German traffic can use the bridge.** The 10th SS goes round via Pannerden. German losses at the bridge on 18 Sep: about 10–12 armoured vehicles and about 70 dead from Gräbner alone, plus 2–3 of Mielke's tanks and heavy infantry losses [MID], [PA-B18].
- **West Arnhem:** the **Sperrlinie Spindler is intact** from N of the Amsterdamseweg (De Leeren Doedel) down the Dreijenseweg to Oosterbeek-Hoog, along the railway to the Heijenoordseweg, and from Den Brink and the Utrechtseweg E of the Elisabeth Gasthuis to the Rhine. An inner line is forming about 800 m from the bridge. 1 and 3 Para are stuck around the Rhine Pavilion / Onderlangs and Lombok, about 1.5 km short of the bridge. The 2nd South Staffords and 11 Para (second lift) are on their way to join them (the 19 Sep dawn attack, outside the window).
- **West of the landing zones:** Tettau holds **Renkum** and the woods E of Wageningen and is pressing Heelsum and DZ "X". Helle has been broken on the Ginkel Heath and the 4th Parachute Brigade has landed. The British still hold the LZs and DZs W of Wolfheze, so Tettau has **not** closed the western ring.
- **North:** Krafft at Schaarsbergen and Allwörden at the Amsterdamseweg block the northern approach. Deelen is in German hands.
- **Command:** Model at Doetinchem with Bittrich. Harzer has the 9th SS sector. Tettau is still under WBN (not under Bittrich until 20 Sep).
- **German victory condition, if modelled:** hold or retake the bridge exit and keep the British from linking 1st Para Bde with Frost. Historically the Germans achieved the second, not the first, by 21:00 18 Sep.

## 14. Open questions and conflicts (priority order)

1. **Clock basis.** Confirm that German times are one hour ahead of British times for each key event: Gräbner's southbound crossing (18:00 German?), the Gräbner attack (09:00–09:30 British), Knaust's arrival (04:00 German?). This decides which turn each event falls in.
2. **Krafft's strength at 13:00:** 306 [MID] or 435 [DA-K, MID after reinforcement]. Who were the extra 129 men, and when did they join?
3. **Gräbner's vehicle types.** The mix of armoured cars, SdKfz 250/251 and trucks in the 22-vehicle attack is not established; Pegasus's "SdKfz 233/234" is doubtful. Losses are given as 10, 11, 12 or "20" (photo captions). Where did the 10 reserve vehicles fight on 17–18 Sep?
4. **Brinkmann's personnel strength and arrival route** (from Borculo via Zutphen and Velp, or via Doetinchem, Zevenaar and Westervoort). No figure was found. The equipment list may be paper strength.
5. **Mielke and Knaust tank numbers and timing:** 8 (2 Pz IV, 6 Pz III), "ten older tanks" [TIE], or 15–16 at departure? Did they arrive at 04:00 with Knaust or later on 18 Sep?
6. **KG Harder:** the time it entered the line on 18 Sep, and whether its "3 tanks" are II./SS-Pz.Rgt.9's 3 Panthers. Harder's rank and first name were not confirmed.
7. **Spindler's strength on 17 Sep:** 120 or 2 × 120. Also the exact trace of the night 17/18 line between Oosterbeek-Hoog and Den Brink, and when the river gap closed.
8. **Tettau's committed strength on 18 Sep.** Pegasus's "3,000" for Lippert is surely the whole school. Which Fliegerhorst battalions were present (identity and commander names are confused between Zaiser, Zaubzer, Merkens and Liebsch)?
9. **Flak:** what was manned at the bridge, at Deelen and at the Koningsweg on 17 Sep, after the morning bombing? The parent unit of the south-bank 88 at Meinerswijk is unknown.
10. **StuG-Brigade 280:** did any vehicle reach the Arnhem area before 21:00 on 18 Sep? All sources found say 19 Sep morning. If the first gun arrived in the night of 18/19, it could be a last-turn reinforcement.
11. **Harzer's forward CP in Arnhem** (building and date) and the exact Doetinchem building of II SS-Pz.Korps. Neither was found.
12. **Euling at Arnhem:** a company group [NAF] or most of the battalion [TIE]? When exactly did it leave for Pannerden on 18 Sep?
