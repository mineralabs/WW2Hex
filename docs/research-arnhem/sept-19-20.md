# Arnhem, 19–20 September 1944: both sides

Research notes for the WW2Hex "Market Garden campaign" scenario, which runs from 15:00 on 17 Sep to
21:00 on 20 Sep 1944. This file adds **19 and 20 September** in the Arnhem / Oosterbeek / Driel area
to the two existing files, which stop at 21:00 on 18 Sep:

- [allied-forces.md](allied-forces.md) (Allied side, 17–18 Sep)
- [german-forces.md](german-forces.md) (German side, 17–18 Sep)

Read those first. Unit ids used here match `scenarios/arnhem-1944/source/scenario.source.json`.

**Status of this file: provisional.** Nothing here should be marked `verified` in scenario data until
the open questions in section 10 are checked.

## 0. How to read this file

- **Confidence labels** (V / P / U) as in the two earlier files: **V** = two or more independent
  sources agree; **P** = one source, or sources that disagree on the detail, or an estimate whose
  basis is stated; **U** = no source found. Where a unit gets three labels they are location /
  strength / equipment.
- **Clock.** **All times are Allied "A" time (GMT+1)**, the clock of the British war diaries.
  German sources (Krafft's and Möller's diaries, Tieke, Fürbringer, Defending Arnhem) use German
  summer time (GMT+2). **German-clock times have been converted one hour earlier** and are marked
  "(German 15:00)" or similar where the original clock matters. Secondary books mix the two, so
  one-hour conflicts are common (see section 10).
- **Coordinates** are WGS84 decimal degrees, good to about ±300 m unless stated. Most come from the
  1944 GSGS 4427 grid references in the war diaries, converted with the fit in allied-forces.md 8.1:
  lat = 51.9877 + (N − 78.3) / 111.2, lon = 5.8327 + (E − 69.3) / 68.7 (for 693783, E = 69.3,
  N = 78.3). Four-figure (square) references are ±700 m. Others are OpenStreetMap Nominatim look-ups
  of the modern feature [OSM], queried Oct 2026, or estimates marked *est.* with their basis.
- **Turns.** Turns are 2 hours. Turn 1 = 15:00 17 Sep, so turn *n* starts at 15:00 17 Sep plus
  2(*n* − 1) hours. Useful values: turn 17 = 23:00 18 Sep; 19 = 03:00 19 Sep; 20 = 05:00;
  21 = 07:00; 22 = 09:00; 23 = 11:00; 24 = 13:00; 25 = 15:00; 26 = 17:00; 27 = 19:00;
  28 = 21:00 19 Sep; 30 = 01:00 20 Sep; 32 = 05:00; 33 = 07:00; 34 = 09:00; 35 = 11:00; 36 = 13:00;
  37 = 15:00; 38 = 17:00; 39 = 19:00 20 Sep (the last turn, ending 21:00). The existing scenario
  file uses the same numbering (its second lift is turn 12 = 13:00 18 Sep; `victory.lastTurn` 15).
- **Source keys** in square brackets are listed with URLs in section 11. (S) marks secondary sources.
- **Light, 19–20 Sep (A time, est.):** sunset about 18:43 and last light about 19:35; first light
  about 05:22, sunrise about 06:16 (estimate: the 17/18 Sep values in allied-forces.md shifted by
  about 2 minutes a day). Turns 27–32 (19:00–07:00) are night turns, as are 39 and the evening part
  of 38. Weather: fine on 19 Sep at Arnhem, but fog and low cloud in England delayed or cancelled
  lifts on both days [PA-3L; RAFM4].

## 1. Summary for scenario design

1. **19 Sep, dawn: the last attempt to reach the bridge fails.** 1 Para, 3 Para (each about 100–150
   men), the 2nd South Staffords and 11 Para attacked east from about 726778 between 04:00 and 04:45,
   1 and 3 Para along the river road (Onderlangs), the Staffords along the Utrechtseweg toward the
   Monastery / Arnhem Museum (734780). 1 Para was broken by about 07:30; the Staffords were overrun by
   tanks (and StuG 280) at the museum at about 10:50–11:00; 11 Para was caught forming up for a
   flank attack about 14:30. **About 440–500 survivors** fell back 3 km west to the Oosterbeek-Laag
   church area by 14:30–16:00, where Lt Col Thompson and Major Lonsdale organised them ("Thompson
   Force", later "Lonsdale Force"). [DIV-R; STAFFS-WD; 1PARA-WD; 11PARA-WD; WIKI-A] (V)
2. **19 Sep: 4th Para Bde stopped on the Dreijenseweg.** 156 Bn attacked the Spindler line on the
   Dreijenseweg from 08:30 and lost half its strength; 10 Bn was held at about 690810. At about
   14:00–14:20 Urquhart ordered the brigade south of the railway; the move began about 15:00–16:00,
   in contact, **while the Polish gliders were landing on LZ L** (about 15:00–16:00). By night 156
   (about 270) and 10 Bn (about 250) were in the woods near Wolfheze, 7 KOSB (about 270 on 20 Sep) at
   the White House / Ommershof [4PB-WD; 156-WD; KOSB-WD; DIV-R] (V).
3. **20 Sep: 4th Para Bde fights its way into the perimeter.** 10 Bn reached Div HQ at 13:10 with
   about 60 men. 156 Bn and Brigade HQ were pinned in "Hackett's hollow" (686787) for most of the day
   and broke in at about 17:00–18:50 with about 50–90 men. B Coy KOSB and parts of 156 were captured
   at Wolfheze [DIV-R; 4PB-WD; 156-WD; WIKI-A] (V).
4. **The Oosterbeek perimeter.** It formed over 19–20 Sep around the Hartenstein (Div HQ), from the
   Border's line on the west (Koude Herberg to the Westerbouwing and Heveadorp) to the KOSB at the
   White House on the north and Lonsdale Force at the church on the south-east. On 21 Sep it was about
   **4.8 km round and held by about 3,600 men** [MID p.339 via WIKI-A; PA-FORM] (P). In 1 km hexes it
   covers about **5–7 hexes** (see section 4.4).
5. **The bridge.** About 740 men on 17/18 Sep; about 140 effective on the evening of 20 Sep, with
   about 250–300 wounded in cellars. Frost was wounded about 13:30–15:45 on 20 Sep and Gough took
   over. A truce of about 2 hours in the evening handed over about 280 wounded. **Tanks crossed the
   bridge north to south at 18:15 on 20 Sep** [DIV-R log]. The last organised resistance ended at
   about **05:00 on 21 Sep**, after the scenario ends [DIV-R paras 105–108; PA-BR20] (V).
   **For the game: the bridge force should normally still exist at 21:00 on 20 Sep but be
   unable to stop German traffic over the bridge from about the 17:00 turn of 20 Sep.**
6. **Polish gliders, LZ L, 19 Sep.** 35 Polish Horsas (plus 8 carry-overs) took off at noon; about
   28 Polish and 2 British gliders arrived. 10 Polish 6-pdrs were flown; 3 were unloaded and **2
   reached Oosterbeek** with about 4 jeeps. 9 of 93 Poles were killed [MID via TFT; PA-3L; LIB-RT].
   The Polish parachute battalions did not fly on 19 or 20 Sep (they dropped at Driel at about 17:00
   on 21 Sep, outside the scenario).
7. **Resupply.** 19 Sep: 163 aircraft, 388 tons net, dropped about 16:00 on SDP "V" (7179, north of
   Oosterbeek-Hoog), which was in German hands; **about 12 tons** collected [DIV-R App. D, E]. 20 Sep:
   about 162–164 aircraft, 386 tons net, in two waves (14:06 and about 17:00) on and around the
   Hartenstein; **about 21 tons** collected per the division, "about half" per Pegasus (conflict)
   [DIV-R; PA-OB20].
8. **The Heveadorp ferry** (686768) was in British hands on 19–20 Sep: Div HQ reported it "still
   retained" at 15:05 on 20 Sep. Capt Heggie's party of 9th Field Coy (about 15) held it from the
   early afternoon of 20 Sep and was driven off **just after midnight 20/21 Sep**; the ferry was lost
   or sunk by 21 Sep. It carried 8 jeeps [BORDER-WD O.O.; 9FD-WD; 1ALB-WD; DIV-R].
9. **German side.** See sections 5–7. The main new arrivals are StuG-Brigade 280 (19 Sep morning),
   Tiger company Hummel (2 Tigers on the evening of 19 Sep, the rest on 20 Sep), and Tettau's
   reinforcements in the west (KG Knoche, Pz.Kp. 224, Btl. Wossowski, KG Bruhn and others). On
   20 Sep Tettau was put under II SS-Panzerkorps and the 9th SS and Tettau began closing the ring
   on Oosterbeek from east and west.

### 2.4 The Polish glider element on LZ L

- **Lift.** 35 Horsas carried the Polish glider element (vehicles and the anti-tank battery). Eight
  more carried British loads left over from earlier lifts: 43 took off **at about noon**, several
  hours late because of fog [PA-3L; MID via TFT]. Seven force-landed in England, the North Sea or
  Belgium; seven were lost over Holland (six with tow ropes cut by flak, one blown up). **About 28
  Polish and 2 British gliders arrived** [MID via TFT]; "about 30" [PA-3L; BOA-3L] (V for about 30).
- **Landing zone:** LZ L, square 6880 [DIV-R para 125]: **about 52.007, 5.821** (square centre),
  north of the railway between Wolfheze and Johannahoeve (Johannahoeve [OSM] 52.008, 5.837).
- **Time (conflict):** about 14:00 [KOSB-WD]; 14:30 [4PSQN-WD]; 15:15 [1ALB-WD]; 15:20 seen passing
  overhead [1GPR-WD]; about 16:00 [DIV-R para 125]; 16:30 [DIV-R log]. With a noon take-off and
  about 2.5–3 hours' flying (the second lift took about the same), **about 15:00–16:00 is the best
  estimate (P)**. **Game: turn 25 (15:00–17:00, 19 Sep).**
- **What arrived** (P): the Polish anti-tank battery's two troops of five 6-pdrs (10 guns) were flown;
  8 arrived [WIKI-A citing Buckingham]; 3 were unloaded serviceable [PA-3L; MID]; **2 guns under
  2/Lt Władysław Mleczko reached Oosterbeek** [MID via TFT], with **4 jeeps and 2 trailers**
  [LIB-RT]. Three Polish pieces are described by the Utrechtseweg / Oranjeweg crossroads near the
  Hartenstein [LIB-AT]; the third may have come with the earlier Polish gliders of 18 Sep (U).
- **Men:** 93 Poles took off; 9 were killed or died of wounds [MID; PA-3L]; 3 missing [LIB-RT]. The
  2nd A/Tk Battery's second-in-command also came in [2ATB-WD]. About 60 glider pilots (2 per
  glider) landed, and joined the GPR (estimate).
- **On the ground** the gliders landed among the withdrawing 4th Para Bde and its pursuers. KOSB's
  B Coy helped unload; there were exchanges of fire between Poles and KOSB [1ALB-WD]. Many loads
  could not be salvaged.
- **Game counter.** One small counter: "Polish A/Tk det", about 40 men, 2 × 6-pdr, wheeled (jeeps),
  arriving turn 25 at LZ L (52.007, 5.821). If the hex is enemy-held or adjacent to enemy, apply the
  landing-loss rule (allied-forces.md 5.2 item 6). Merge the glider pilots into a GPR counter or
  ignore them.

### 2.5 Frost's bridge force, 19 September

- **Strength.** 740 at the start [MSW; PA-BR19]. By 19 Sep evening "up to 150" wounded in the
  cellars [PA-BR19]. *Estimate*: about 400–450 effective at nightfall 19 Sep (740 less the losses
  needed to reach about 140 effective by the evening of 20 Sep, spread over two days) (U: no return
  found).
- **03:00:** Germans forming up by the Van Limburg Stirum School are bombed with grenades; about 20
  killed [PA-BR19].
- **Midday:** 3 tanks shell houses from near the river; three PIAT hits and they withdraw, though two
  more arrive [DIV-R para 97; PA-BR19 (late morning, Mk IIIs)].
- **Afternoon and evening:** continuous bombardment; buildings "systematically levelled"; phosphorus;
  by evening **at least five houses burned down, including a key one just east of the bridge**
  [DIV-R para 98]. **Tigers** fire into the NE houses in the evening (see section 6) [PA-BR19].
- Houses held: the Van Limburg Stirum school (1st Para Sqn RE and 3 Para men); Bde HQ west of the
  ramp; A Coy around the ramp; "Map Ref 24" (RAOC) was the only position given up on 19 Sep. The
  Germans held all houses north and west, covering the position by fire [DIV-R para 99; PA-BR19].
- Ammunition "now acute"; 2 Para could not afford to return fire [DIV-R para 99]. One 6-pdr lost
  in a trap set for a Tiger [PA-BR19].
- Footprint: still the single hex at the north end of the bridge (allied-forces.md 4.5) (V).

## 3. Allied: 20 September

### 3.1 4th Para Bde breaks into the perimeter

  (the existing scenario note already makes it a crossing). Historically it was British-controlled
  on 19–20 Sep, but nothing large crossed. The Poles were not yet at Driel.

## 6. German: the bridge, Tettau's advance and the Sperrlinie

### 6.1 The bridge, 19–20 Sep (German side)

- **Attackers:** KG Brinkmann (SS-Pz.Aufkl.Abt. 10: armoured cars and SPWs), **KG Knaust** with
  Pz.Kp. Mielke (Pz III/IV), Sonnenstuhl's reception post, the 10th SS howitzer battery firing
  over open sights from the gardens, and from the evening of 19 Sep **Hummel's Tigers**
  (german-forces.md 4; [TIE; DA-HUM]).
- **19 Sep:** about 03:00 a group forming up by the school lost about 20 killed to grenades
  [PA-BR19]. At dawn or midday 3 Pz III attacked the eastern sector; one knocked out by Capt Frank's
  PIAT, the others withdrew [PA-BR19; DIV-R para 97]. Through the afternoon, systematic destruction
  of houses by tank and artillery fire, with phosphorus; in the evening two Tigers fired from the
  Nijmeegseweg ramp into the houses on the Eusebiusbuitensingel [DA-HUM; PA-BR19]. A surrender demand
  was carried in by a British prisoner (L/Sgt Halliwell); Frost did not reply [PA-BR19].
- **20 Sep:** Brinkmann, Knaust and Aufkl.Abt. 10 used flame-throwers and Panzerfausts [TIE].
  Engineers tried twice to lay charges on the ramp arches; Grayburn's counter-attacks drove them
  off and British sappers removed the fuses [PA-BR20; DIV-R log 17:10]. A surrender demand in the
  afternoon was refused [TIE]. The school fell in the afternoon after Tiger fire. **German armour
  crossed the bridge north to south from the late afternoon** (4 Tigers and a recce vehicle at
  18:15 A [DIV-R log]; "4 or 5 tanks" [DIV-R para 104]).
- **Evening 20 Sep:** two-hour truce; the Germans carried off about 280 British wounded, mostly to
  the St Elisabeth Hospital, and edged closer to Bde HQ meanwhile [PA-BR20].
- **End:** British resistance ceased about 05:00 21 Sep [DIV-R para 107; WIKI-A]. Tieke has
  Brinkmann taking "the bitterly contested bunker on the northern approach" at about 10:00 A (German
  11:00) on 21 Sep and clearing one lane through Gräbner's wrecks [TIE]. **Knaust's armoured group
  crossed "shortly after midday" on 21 Sep** (German clock), lead elements due at Elst about 16:00
  [TIE IV]. Nafziger's "Knaust departed Arnhem at noon, arrives Elst by 1600 on 20 September" [NAF]
  is probably the same event misdated by a day (conflict).
- **Reading for the game:** single vehicles could and did cross on the evening of 20 Sep; organised
  German traffic over the bridge began about 11:00–12:00 A on 21 Sep, after the scenario ends.

### 6.2 Tettau's advance from the west

- **19 Sep:** light probes against 1 Border, "easily beaten off" [PA-FORM]; Border companies were
  "attacked lightly" about 19:00 [BORDER-WD]. Eberwein reached Wolfheze along the railway and was
  stopped when the Luftwaffe strafed it [MG7]. Knoche and Pz.Kp. 224 came east along the
  Amsterdamseweg from the Ginkel Heath [MG7]. Lippert's battalions and 10. SStA were around Heelsum
  and Renkum after the fights of 18 Sep (german-forces.md 5); no 19 Sep detail found for them (U).
  Dutch Resistance reports of "a large formation" closing from the west made 4th Para Bde take the
  Wolfheze crossing rather than the Oosterbeek-Hoog one [PA-3L].
- **Night 19/20 Sep:** Knoche linked with Krafft's right, closing the north side [DA-K].
- **20 Sep:** Tettau's units passed through Krafft by 07:30 A (German 08:30) [DA-K]. B Coy KOSB
  and parts of 156 were captured at Wolfheze in the afternoon [PA-FORM]. The Border's positions were
  shelled (1 AL Bde HQ hit at 08:30), A Coy attacked by infantry and an SP gun at 10:00, and C Coy at
  Koude Herberg by flame-thrower tanks and an SP gun at 15:00 and again at 19:30 [BORDER-WD;
  1ALB-WD; PA-FORM]. Wossowski swept the Ginkel Heath woods and moved to Wolfheze [DA-HG].
- **Night 20/21 Sep:** about midnight an unnamed German force drove Heggie's party off the Heveadorp
  ferry [9FD-WD; WIKI-A]. The Westerbouwing fell to Wossowski on the morning of 21 Sep (outside the
  window).
- **Command:** Tettau was **placed under II SS-Panzerkorps on 20 Sep** [NAF; MID p.341 via
  german-forces.md]. Model ordered all units round the pocket under the 9th SS for a concentric
  attack at 07:00 A on 21 Sep (German 08:00, clock?) [DA-HG].

### 6.3 The Sperrlinie on 19–20 Sep

- **Line:** as in german-forces.md 8.2: north of the Amsterdamseweg, down the **Dreijenseweg**
  (52.008–51.996, 5.846–5.842) to **Oosterbeek-Hoog station** ([OSM] 51.995, 5.840), then east
  along the north side of the railway to Heijenoord (51.990, 5.877), with blocks at the museum /
  St Elisabeth Gasthuis (51.985, 5.890) and the river harbour [BOA-S18; PA-4P19].
- **Sub-groups:** Allwörden (Dreijenseweg / Amsterdamseweg), Harder (Oosterbeek station to the
  Rhine), Möller (Utrechtseweg), Spindler's gunners, Krafft (north), Bruhn (north, from 19–20 Sep),
  the 9th SS SP flak company (north of Oosterbeek) and the PGR 19/20 alarm companies (Rhine bank,
  attacked by 1 Para at 05:00 on 19 Sep) [DA-280; MG8; NAF].
- **Against 4th Para Bde on 19 Sep:** the sources say only "Sperrverband Spindler", with armoured
  cars, SP guns and dual-purpose flak along the Dreijenseweg [PA-4P19]. Allwörden is the documented
  unit on that road (inference, P), with the SP flak company and 3 StuGs from the morning [DA-280;
  MG8].
- **Against the western Arnhem attack on 19 Sep:** Möller (with 3 StuGs) on the Utrechtseweg, Harder
  (3 StuGs) and the PGR 19/20 companies by the river, Gropp's flak north of the railway, 20-mm and
  88-mm fire from Meinerswijk on the south bank (51.975, 5.884) and Gräbner's survivors in the
  south-bank brickworks [DA-PI9; MG8; BOA-UW].
- **19 Sep afternoon to 20 Sep:** the line moved west behind the retreating British. By the evening
  of 20 Sep it ran against the east face of the perimeter: from the railway at Oosterbeek-Hoog,
  down the Stationsweg / Utrechtseweg crossroads (705783) to the Oosterbeek-Laag church area. Möller
  led assault groups down the Utrechtseweg with RAD, naval and Luftwaffe reinforcements; the
  Schoonoord MDS was taken about 11:00 [DA-PI9; DIV-R].

### 6.4 The German view at 21:00 on 20 Sep

- **Bridge:** won in practice. The British remnant (about 140) was surrounded around Bde HQ with its
  wounded surrendered; tanks were crossing. Clearing the north ramp and pushing Knaust's armour to
  Elst against XXX Corps was the next task [TIE; DIV-R].
- **Nijmegen:** the Waal bridge fell to the Guards and US 82nd Airborne at about 19:00 on 20 Sep
  [TIE III] (Div HQ heard at midnight [DIV-R log]). This moved the 10th SS's attention, and
  Landstorm Nederland and Knaust's armour, south.
- **Oosterbeek:** the British were in a pocket ("Kessel") about 2 km across around the Hartenstein,
  with their back to the Rhine. The 9th SS (Spindler's groups, StuG 280) pressed from the east and
  north-east, Krafft and Knoche had closed the north, and Tettau (now under Bittrich) from the west.
  The **ferry and the Westerbouwing** were the next objectives; they fell on the night of 20/21 and
  morning of 21 Sep. I found no direct Harzer or Bittrich quote for 20 Sep; Kershaw is the likely
  source (U).
- **Resupply:** most of the 19 and 20 Sep drops fell into German hands (SDP V; LZ Z), which eased
  German food and ammunition shortages [PA-3L; PA-OB20].

### 9.3 Suggested victory conditions for the campaign (Arnhem sector)

The campaign scenario covers the whole corridor, so these are the Arnhem-sector terms only. They
extend allied-forces.md 7.2.

1. **Bridge north end.** VP per turn held (as before). Historically held to the end of turn 39 only
   in a contested sense. *Suggest*: Allied VP if an Allied unit is in the north-end hex at the end of
   turn 39 (historically: yes, just). German VP if a German unit has **crossed the bridge** (entered
   the south-end hex from the north) by the end (historically: yes, 18:15 on 20 Sep).
2. **Link-up.** Large Allied VP if a supplied Allied unit from outside the bridge force (or a XXX
   Corps unit) reaches the north-end hex or an adjacent hex. Historically failed.
3. **Oosterbeek base.** Allied VP if the Hartenstein and church hexes are Allied at the end
   (historically yes). Extra if the **Heveadorp ferry hex** is Allied (historically yes, until
   midnight). German VP for each of these hexes held.
4. **XXX Corps at the Neder Rijn.** In the full campaign, a large Allied bonus if any XXX Corps
   unit reaches the south bank opposite Arnhem or Driel by 21:00 20 Sep. Historically the Nijmegen
   bridge fell only at about 19:00 on 20 Sep and no ground unit reached the Neder Rijn until 22 Sep
   (a Phantom report at 16:00 on 20 Sep of recce on the south bank was false) [DIV-R log].
5. **Losses.** As before. Historically the 1st Airborne lost about 60% of its strength by 21:00
   20 Sep (about 3,600 effective in the perimeter plus 140 at the bridge, out of about 10,000 landed
   including glider pilots; *estimate*).
6. **Calibration.** At 21:00 on 20 Sep the historical result is a clear **German operational win
   at Arnhem** (bridge lost to traffic, division penned in a pocket) with the Allies keeping a
   viable bridgehead (the perimeter and the ferry). Set thresholds so that this scores as a German
   minor or marginal victory in the Arnhem sector, leaving the overall campaign result to the
   Nijmegen and corridor sectors.

## 10. Open questions and conflicts

In priority order.

1. **Polish glider landing time:** 14:00, 14:30, 15:15, 15:20, 16:00 or 16:30 in different British
   diaries. Chosen: turn 25 (15:00). Check Middlebrook, Peters & Buist (*Glider Pilots at Arnhem*)
   and the Polish sources.
2. **Polish guns:** 10 flown, 8 arrived (Buckingham), 3 unloaded, 2 reached Oosterbeek (Middlebrook);
   a Polish source shows 3 pieces by the Hartenstein. How many Polish 6-pdrs came on 18 Sep?
3. **20 Sep resupply collected:** 21 tons (Div App. E), 13% (Wikipedia, citing Evans) or "about
   half" (Pegasus). 19 Sep: 12 tons (Div) or 31 short tons (Evans). The Div figures are official but
   admit "about another 100 tons" was used unrecorded. Exact SDP V position (only the square 7179
   is given).
4. **StuG 280 arrival** time and route (unloading station not found). The split 3/3/3 (DA) or all
   10 with Krafft (Pegasus).
5. **Hummel's second group:** arrival time on 20 Sep (not found) and route (Westervoort or Velp).
   Did any Tigers reach Oosterbeek on 20 Sep (no source says so)?
6. **Bridge timing:** Frost wounded at about 13:30 (Wikipedia) or reported 15:45 (Div log). The
   truce's clock time. When did German traffic first cross: single tanks 18:15 20 Sep (Div log) and
   Tigers "on the 20th" (DA), against Knaust's organised crossing midday 21 Sep (Tieke) and
   Nafziger's "noon 20 Sep" (probably misdated).
7. **Bridge strength on 19 Sep:** no return between 740 (18 Sep) and 140 (evening 20 Sep). The
   400–450 used here is an estimate.
8. **Lonsdale Force:** when Lonsdale took command (late 19 Sep or 10:30–14:00 20 Sep).
9. **10 Bn's position on the evening of 20 Sep:** crossroads 705783 or 696784 (600 m apart: maybe
   different hexes).
10. **156 Bn's strength after the hollow:** 50, 60, 70 or 90.
11. **German arrivals with no time:** Knoche and Pz.Kp. 224 (19 Sep), Bruhn (19 or 20 Sep), the
    naval and police units with Krafft (19 Sep), Swoboda's batteries and their sites. Kershaw,
    Fürbringer and Zwarts (*German Armour in Arnhem*) should settle most of these.
12. **Pz.Kp. 224's strength and losses:** 17 tanks (one root), about 8 by the cauldron phase; were
    six Renaults lost at Renkum on 18 Sep or later?
13. **Clock basis** of DA times ("2000h" Tigers, "1600" Krafft attack, "1000h" StuG): assumed German
    and converted; if they are British, each is an hour later in A time.
14. **The Heveadorp ferry's fate:** sunk by the ferryman (Ryan) or abandoned listing with the cable
    gear jammed (Buckingham); the Div report says sunk. Either way it was unusable from about
    midnight 20/21 Sep.
15. **Several grid references in the diaries look mis-transcribed** (3 Para "706744", 1 Para's church
    "677773", DIV-R paras 120, 145, 155). They have been read as the obvious corrections (706774,
    696773, 685799, 670799, 702784–703774), P.
16. **Perimeter strength 3,600:** one root (Middlebrook), repeated by Pegasus and Wikipedia.
