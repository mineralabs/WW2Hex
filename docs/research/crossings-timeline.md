### How I sourced this
- **history.army.mil returned 403.** I used the HyperWar (ibiblio) mirror of the official CMH history, C.B. MacDonald, *The Siegfried Line Campaign*:

  - Chapter VIII (19–21 Sep): https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USA/USA-E-Siegfried/USA-E-Siegfried-8.html, cited below as **[MacD-8]**.
  - I treat these as authoritative.

Index: https://erenow.org/ww/nijmegen-us-82nd-airborne-division-1944/. ### Minor watercourses
- **Niers bridge, Gennep:** in German hands in Sep 1944, so presumably intact then. That is my inference. The Germans blew it while retreating around 11 Feb 1945, and 51st Highland Division built a Bailey bridge, later named "Highlander Bridge". Sources: https://www.gennep.nl/en/niers-bridge-1945-honorary-name-highlander-bridge and https://www.europeremembers.com/pois/784/highlander-bridge. Status: 1945 demolition V; Sep 1944 status P (inferred).
- **Ooijpolder streams, the Wetering/Linge near Elst, and the Groesbeek/Wyler streams:** I found nothing on bridges or obstacles there. Status: U.

### XXX Corps route, Grave to Nijmegen, 19 Sep (V)
1. **08:20:** B Sqn, 2nd Household Cavalry armoured cars reach Grave bridge.
2. **About 10:00:** the leading Grenadier Guards Shermans reach Grave.
3. They are diverted because Honinghutje was reported damaged.
4. Route: Grave bridge → north via **Overasselt** (vehicles queued there late morning) → **Heumen lock bridge** → left towards **Malden** → halt to reorganise at **De Kluis** → north, then right towards **Sionshof** → across the Groesbeekseweg into Nijmegen.

Sources:
- Diversion via Overasselt and Heumen: [Saun-4] and [Saun-5].
- Heumen as the column's crossing: [MacD-7] and [MacD-8].
- Malden, De Kluis and Sionshof legs: the TracesOfWar Grenadier Guards plaque snippet only (https://www.tracesofwar.com/sights/12273/Plaque-Grenadier-Guardsviaduct.htm), so **P**.
