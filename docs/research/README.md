# Research for the Nijmegen scenario

Gathered on 16 October 2026 from published histories and open map data, for the Stage 4 scenario.
All of it is provisional: much was read through online copies or quotations, and the key printed
works have not yet been checked (each file lists its open questions).

| [crossings-timeline.md](crossings-timeline.md) | Every Maas, canal and Waal crossing and its state on 17-20 September; the 19-20 September timeline |

| [map-sources.md](map-sources.md) | Historical and modern map and elevation sources with their licences |
