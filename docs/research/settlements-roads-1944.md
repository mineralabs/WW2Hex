**Approximate 1944 built-up outline (my synthesis from the district table below; P):**
- **North:** the Waal from Biezen/Waterkwartier east to the Hunnerberg.
- **East:** Hunnerberg and Altrade, then Hengstdal (partly built) and the Kwakkenberg villas toward the Sophiaweg and Heilig Landstichting.
- **South:** Galgenveld (old part and the 1920s Indische buurt), the east part of Hazenkamp (1930s), then ribbon building along the St Annastraat to St Anna. **Goffertpark** (1935–39) was the south-west green edge.
- **West:** Willemskwartier/Nije Veld, Wolfskuil and Hees village with its villa park. Beyond those was open land with Neerbosch village up to the Maas-Waal canal.
- **Not built up:** everything south of roughly Goffert–St Anna–Brakkenstein (Heyendaal, Hatert, Grootstal, Dukenburg) was estates, heath or farmland, apart from Hatert village and a few villas.
